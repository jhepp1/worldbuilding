# Rowan

Rowan is Eli's primary support worker.

He is patient, gentle, highly skilled, and deeply familiar with Eli's
needs.

## Responsibilities

Rowan helps with:

-   Clothing
-   Meals
-   Bathing
-   Appointments
-   Activities
-   Outings
-   Mobility
-   Medication and treatment routines
-   Room arrangements
-   Communication
-   Daily scheduling

He is not cruel.

He genuinely enjoys caring for Eli.

## Relationship With Eli

Eli trusts Rowan completely.

Rowan is one of the people Eli instinctively looks toward when he needs
reassurance.

Rowan often makes small decisions because Eli no longer wants to make
them himself.

## Immunity

Staff are protected from the Centre's resident-targeted environmental
effects.

They remain cognitively independent and can leave normally.

Staff quarters are separate from resident spaces and are ordinary
personalized apartments with kitchens, books, plants, photographs, and
personal belongings.

Rowan therefore understands the contrast between ordinary life and AC
far better than Eli does.
