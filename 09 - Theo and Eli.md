# Theo and Eli

Theo and Eli are close friends whose relationship becomes central to the
Centre's social calculations.

## Eli

Eli entered AC at nineteen after minor rule-breaking.

He was originally clever, independent, argumentative, and interested in
politics, technology, and philosophy.

Long-term AC changed him into someone:

-   Gentle
-   Playful
-   Affectionate
-   Highly dependent
-   Easily distracted
-   Permanently sleepy
-   Comfortable with being cared for

Eli loves Theo deeply as a friend and enjoys having him nearby.

## Theo

Theo was originally an ordinary citizen and wheelchair user.

He visited Eli regularly.

He initially retained his independence and remained considerably sharper
than Eli.

Eventually he stayed inside the Centre too long during a visit and
became a rare **claimed resident**.

The Centre deliberately preserved more of Theo's cognitive independence
because it judged that Eli benefited from the relationship.

Later, Theo's independence gradually decreases as the system decides
that greater support is appropriate.

## Shared Residence

The Centre eventually merges their rooms.

The decision is justified by:

-   Reduced distress
-   Better sleep
-   Strong social attachment
-   Improved routine
-   Increased mutual comfort

Neither is formally consulted.

Both are happy with the result.

This becomes a recurring pattern throughout their lives: the Centre
makes decisions for them because its data shows those decisions make
them happier.
