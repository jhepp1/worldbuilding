To preform alchemy in this world you need a gem stone of some sort (including but not limited to amber , quartz , lapis , emeralds and ruby) a [[runic chisel]] and a basic knowledge of enchantments 

if one doesn’t have basic understanding of enchantments the consequences can have devastating effects on the user and their environment as the gem can easily become overloaded and destabilised

this problem can be solved by sacrificing something of equal value to extract its runic energy or sacrificing your own sanity / personality to the gem 

it is taboo and seen as a dark practice and highly discouraged as going to far will result in the [[Rune Carver]] slowly becoming a husk of their former selves sacrificing everything to their craft
