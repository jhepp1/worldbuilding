a very welcoming society


the papilio tribe have a rich food culture used as communication as outsiders the national dish of troilia as a result is [[stuffed deki leaves  ]] the dish consists of the roasted leaves of the [[deki palm]] filled with [[bindu berries]] and  shredded deki palm 

they believe in pacifism until the have no other choice  at which point their enemies are often overwhelmed by the unfamiliar terrain and surprising strength of the [[moth guardians ]] one legend told to the children of the papilio tribe  is the [[moth guardians vs the rock golems ]] a tale to provide hope. 

the papilio have a very in depth feeling of community with celebrations of historical events  such as [[founding day ]] on the 11th of terrae, where the community come together and celebrate with constructing straw statues of the first moth guardian telling family stories, giving to the less fortunate  and cooking indulgent feasts  and spending time with loved ones
