A cult dedicated to the sowing of chaos and violence in the name of The [[Emissary of discord Nthlotep]] they have outposts in all of taranvesh but they are most notorious in Mastonia for being one of the main crime syndicates 

They are said to use weapons forged from steel mixed with blood of [[the last apostle]] this special alloy is said to enhance any runes and enchantments applied to the weapons allow even ordinary bugs to use the powers of the ornarta they both revere and despise 

The brotherhood is theorised to be responsible for some of the darkest days of taranvesh such the as the release of the [[stone golems]] on [[Troilia]] a problem that nearly wiped out the moth clans that make up the papilio tribe in ancient times
