a tribe of long splintered faction of former moth guardians the [[Silk Weavers]] live on the western coast of Troilia in dense self made fortresses of thickly laid silk strands in an attempt to keep everyone away as a result of their cultures superstitions many outsiders mistake these strands for the webs of the [[Arachnie Collective ]] often scaring them off before they even get close to breeching the home of the Silk Weavers

the Silk Weavers keep a good record of their history the silk moths split from the guardians on the 3rd of  Taban 1029 after some intense infighting  that the guardians destroyed all records of this event as they wanted to maintain the guise of a poised and noble institution 

the weavers are much more docile compared to their former companions often taking in the lost and forgotten accepting bugs of most species with little resistance as long as the abide by the community precincts 

they are renowned for creating some of the finest garments in all of Taranvesh
