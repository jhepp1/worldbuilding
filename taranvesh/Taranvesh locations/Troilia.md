homelands of the [[Papilio tribe ]] with a long forgotten history  a  dense humid marshland littered with the broken remains of [[stone golems ]]overtaken by nature and life 

the dense marsh is home to a explosion of native species ranging from moths to caterpillars they inhabit all available space ranging from [[hanging cocoons ]]to huge underground [[burrows]]   Troilia is bountiful in natural resources and rich in colours and natural beauty

Troilia is also the home to the [[Orb of Oros]] a natural gem carved with the [[enchanting rune]] Oros with the power to create abundant and sentient life it is said in [[legends of old]] that the world originates from Troilia  as the first bugs to gain sentience were the Papilio tribe before the records of recorded history 5000+ years ago 


Troilia is dotted with fossils of ancient species the greatest scholars theorise about one such example is a [[human]] a bipedal giant that they say could reach the greatest structures of the taranveshi and has destructive powers lost to time.

the landscape is hard to navigate for anyone unfamiliar with the terrain which makes incredibly difficult to invade especially to grounded bugs who just sink into the mud leading to the history of Troilia being relatively  peaceful over all recorded history however this wasn't always the case, in pre-reordered  history Troilia was invaded by a horde of stone golems many were lost but the moth guardians manged to save the blossoming Papilio tribe leading to them becoming regarded as heroes 

the landscape is still scarred with remnants of this battle such as the remains of shattered golems overtaken by moss and native plants  and large hulking weapons  left standing where their masters had been slain  to the average bugs they look like towering monuments 

the marsh is great for those who call it home providing ample resources and unique materials that make all developed cultures on this land mass completely unlike any other country in the world of Taranvesh  

once you get far into the dense marsh it gives way into a cleared grove full of the winding tunnels of young Papilio on the ground making it quite uneven but fertile ready for farming then above in the tangled vegetation cocoons of the elders of the Papilio tribe hang  blowing gently in the wind  in the perfect position to watch over the young 

the only built up structure in the immediate vicinity of the papilio tribe is the [[Vesperta Spire]] of the Moth Guardians ready to protect the papilio in their times of need although they detest the [[Silk Weavers]]

the main way of life for those in Troilia is a farming and land taming due to the very fertile land and blessing oros  crops grow supernaturally fast hence they are the agricultural superpower of taranvesh and has trade routes with a majority of other nations such as [[Halvelen]] despite the almost global taboo of working directly with Ornarta  as they do not believe in such things 

those in higher power being [[Rune Carvers ]] and blacksmiths 

the climate is consistent year round making it a tourist destination for flying bugs year round a constant mild  [[Lampris]] like weather with great opportunities to look at the cosmos due to its less built up infrastructure compared to the other nations of taranvesh  so it is a dream spot for many astrologists and oracles alike
