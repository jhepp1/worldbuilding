The desert nation, home of the [[Scorpis]] and [[antlion]] species, this area is scarred by what once was.

Ruined technology litters this arid landscape, much too large to be for bugs. 

The main settlement of Rustionia is the river metropolis of [[Plumblossum]] situated on [[the great banks]] of the [[Duian river]] streets lined with polished limestone with thin capillary-like channels carrying water towards an ever-present central chamber that is the crown jewel of the Scorpis as it contains the [[Disus opal]] a giant opal carved with the rune of Disus, capable of transmutation of water into a myriad of other materials at the downside of creating a near-inhospitable local climate.

Due to the way the central chamber is constructed, the water condenses into a dense fog, constantly transmuting into different elements like a sky full of fireworks. 

The central chamber is known by many names, most commonly known as the [[veil between worlds]] or the [[celestial tear of Glacius]]  as it is said to have fallen from [[Stovangrad]] itself when [[high lord Glacius]] fought [[lord of cinder Lumus]] forming a grand crater in the centre of Rustionia, said to be the gateway to infinite worlds.
(Grand in bug perspective but is actually a small crater about 200 cm deep, 100 wide. )

Weather in this locale is extremely hot and dry, often reaching beyond 40oC with blasts of intense winds from the neighbouring [[Shattered lands]] creating an environment hostile to those who are not adapted to the elements, being an [[ornarta]] or blessed by the [[higher ones]] this leads to little being known about [[Rustionia]] outside of folk tales and legends or the occasional pilgriming scorpion.

Leading to an air of mystery to the rest of Taranvesh, as few documents or materials ever surface outside of its borders. 

Due to the harsh soil and lack of nutrients, Plumblossum is the pioneer of [[hydroponics]]
And have dense underground facilities full of hydroponic bays capable of feeding the entire population. The favoured crop is called [[Dussle wheat]] a hearty grain that can be dehydrated in the desert sun for long-term storage; it is used to create an assortment of meals from [[spicy ember syrup porridge]] to soft [[ dussle loafs]]. As a result, a majority of the meals’ flavours come from spices such as [[emberlilly]] an orange flower said to bring a unique flavour to most savoury dishes; as a result, it is highly valued by chefs and the black markets of Taranvesh.
