The dessert nation home of the [[Scorpis]] and [[antlion]] species this area is scarred by what once was 

ruined technology litters this arid landscape much too large to be for bugs 

the main settlement of Rustionia is the  river metropolis of [[Plumblossum]] situated on [[the great banks]] of the [[Duian river]] streets lined with polished limestone with thin capillary like channels carrying water towards an ever present central chamber that is the crown jewel of the scorpis as it contains the [[Disus opal]] a giant opal carved wit the rune of disus capable of transmutation of water into a myriad of other materials at the downside of creating an near inhospitable local climate

Due to the way the central chamber is constructed the water condenses into a dense fog constantly transmuting into different elements like a sky full of fireworks 

The central chamber is known by many names most commonly known as the [[veil between worlds ]] or the [[celestial tear of Glacius]]  as it said to have been fallen from [[stovangrad]] itself when [[high lord Glacius ]] fought  [[lord of cinder lumus]] forming a grand crater in the centre of rustonia said to be the gateway to infinite worlds
(Grand in bug perspective but is actually a small crater about 200 cm deep 100 wide )

weather in this local is extremely hot and dry often reaching beyond 40oC with blast of intense winds from the neighbouring [[Shattered lands]] creating an environment hostile to those who are not adapted to the elements, being an [[ornarta]] or blessed by the [[higher ones]] this leads to little being known about [[Rustionia]] outside of folk tales and legends or the occasional pilgriming scorpion

Leading to an air of mystery to the rest of Taranvesh as few documents or materials ever surface outside of its borders 

Due to the harsh soil and lack of nutrients Plumblossum is the pioneer of [[hydroponics]]
And have dense underground facilities full of hydroponic bays capable of feeding the entire population, the favoured crop is called [[Dussle wheat]] a hearty grain that can be dehydrated in the desert sun for long term storage it is used to create a assortment of meals from [[spicy ember syrup porridge]] to soft [[ dussle loafs]] as a result a majority of the meals flavours comes from spices such as [[emberlilly]] an orange flower that said to bring a unique flavour to most savoury dishes as a result it is highly valued by chefs and and the black markets of taranvesh 

Due to the [[Disus opal]] buildings are often more affordable than they look as they only take up to 72 hours to be constructed from transmuted materials, leading to the scorpions and antlions having a much higher quality of life compared to the average bug in taranvesh with easy access to education and health care 


Due to their beliefs in the higher ones the believe ornarta are sent from Stovangrad it’s as a result any ornarta found within plumblossum are taken to live in the [[palace of Lumus ]] in an elevated life of luxury 

the palace of Lumus is a grand pyramid situated in the centre of rustionia home to many of the scorpis and antlion ornarta and nobility it is a very opulent structure using the long forgotten technology to make it near impenetrable due to the mass congregation of Ornarta like [[Halvelen]] they speak the [[elevated]] language of taranvesh

This particular group ornarta known as the [[skůn]]  have an affinity for carving runes onto themselves adding more power onto their already latent abilities sacrificing their own sanity to do so
