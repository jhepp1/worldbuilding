 [[Mastonia]] - the floating Nation using the natural Avon signet negates the effects of gravity, home of the [[illumiflies]] a very isolated and secretive group , quite recently founded 75 years ago, some native groups worship [[the great tempest]] ( rain is assumed to be the act of a deity) and the light , very clear rituals around a feast some illumiflies become zealots of the light known as the [[orea]] or [[blighted ones]]

A majority Mastonians are very patriotic often reciting the national anthem at important events such as council meetings, school events and festival celebrating mastonian culture however a growing minority are disagreeing with the the traditions of old

The major export of Mastonia is a material called [[aerolite steel]] a light but dense ,silvery blue metal said to be weakly enchanted with the energy of the [[Avon signet]]  leading to the development of a modern air travel industry for grounded and flying  bugs alike  however this has also led to the development of vehicles for warfare eg [[warships ]] and [[divers (vehicle)]] that they also outsource to the highest bidder 

Mastonia  is also the origin of written language in Taranvesh with the language of [[kejish]] which still influences the common bug language today 

However it isn’t all sunshine and rainbows as Mastonia has a large criminal underworld with cartels running districts of the city the largest of which being [[the solarium]] known for producing and distributing the highly addictive substance known as [[morning dew]] made by combining [[distilled sunlight]] with the [[water of a high storm]] other cartels include the [[lunar hand brotherhood ]] , and the [[novae risen triad ]] 

[[High storms ]] are a weather event unique to Mastonia due to its high humidity and presence of the Avon signet a swirling vortex of dark green clouds, mysterious light and heavy rainfall said to have mystical properties as a result buildings are built to withstand the high winds and rain that often bathe this land eg building out of [[granite]] or building into mountains and cliff faces creating opulent structures and grand bunkers 

The average bug in Mastonia lives in the [[communal palacade ]] a grand structure with many branching paths (TBC) 

While the wealthy and elite have personal [[palacades]] for their family usually more ornate and comfortable than the community one, personal ones typical materials include [[marshwood]] ,  [[sunlitum]], [[solar slate]] along with the finest moss or [[pica chic down ]] carpets to create a comfortable luxury few can afford 

Other important structures/ locations include [[the grand distillery ]], [[the giants mirror ]], [[the grand sky gardens]]  [[the weather vein]]
