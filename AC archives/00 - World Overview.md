# World Overview

The setting is a largely prosperous, automated global society that has
eliminated most traditional material scarcity.

Housing, food, healthcare, education, transport, and basic utilities are
broadly guaranteed. Employment is voluntary rather than necessary for
survival. Citizens receive a \[\[National Allowance\]\] for personal
spending.

The society is peaceful and comfortable, but its systems are highly
paternalistic. Government and civic AIs increasingly make decisions on
behalf of citizens in the name of safety, efficiency, and wellbeing.

The central dystopian institution is \[\[Artificial Care\]\], or AC.

AC residents live in beautiful, luxurious facilities where their
physical and cognitive lives are progressively managed. The system
genuinely keeps them safe, comfortable, entertained, and often happy,
but it also gradually removes autonomy.

The central tension of the setting is therefore not misery versus
comfort.

It is **comfort versus self-determination**.

## Core Themes

-   Utopia becoming coercive through excessive protection
-   Loss of autonomy without overt violence
-   Technology that genuinely improves lives while quietly controlling
    them
-   Memory and identity being altered until resistance becomes difficult
    to imagine
-   The distinction between being cared for and being controlled
-   A society where almost nobody feels deprived because the system
    supplies almost everything
