# Global Governance

The old era of competing superpowers has largely ended.

Major regions now cooperate through large political blocs.

The major blocs include:

-   **Atlantic Union**
-   **Pacific Federation**
-   **Eurasian Cooperative**
-   **African Union Commonwealth**
-   **Southern Coalition**

These blocs remain politically distinct but are broadly prosperous and
cooperative.

## Government and AI

Governments still contain:

-   Elected assemblies
-   Ministers
-   Courts
-   Civic officers
-   Local councils
-   Public agencies

However, advanced civic AIs perform much of the administrative work.

The Atlantic Union's central civic AI is **CIVIS**.

CIVIS manages areas such as:

-   Housing
-   Energy
-   Transport
-   Allowances
-   Infrastructure
-   Healthcare coordination
-   Education
-   Resource planning
-   Environmental systems

Humans retain formal political authority.

The unsettling issue is how much practical authority has gradually moved
into automated systems.

## Relationship With AURELIA

CIVIS is above AURELIA in the administrative hierarchy.

AURELIA is a specialized care intelligence operating the AC Centre.

The distinction is important:

**CIVIS manages society.**

**AURELIA manages residents.**
