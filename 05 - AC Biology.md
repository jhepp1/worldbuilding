# AC Biology

Long-term AC treatment gradually rewrites aspects of a resident's
fictional biology.

The changes are not limited to appearance.

## Visible Changes

Common signs include:

-   Very pale, uniform skin
-   Smooth or slightly waxen complexion
-   Fine hair
-   Translucent-looking nails
-   Cool hands and fingertips
-   Glassy-looking eyes
-   Large-looking irises
-   Heavy eyelids
-   Reduced facial coloration
-   Softened facial contours
-   Very quiet breathing
-   Distinctive relaxed posture

## Metabolic Changes

AC residents can develop altered:

-   Appetite
-   Digestion
-   Hydration
-   Waste production
-   Body temperature regulation
-   Sleep cycles
-   Circulation
-   Stress responses

Their bodies become increasingly adapted to a highly managed
environment.

Food is processed efficiently and waste becomes less frequent and
unusually uniform.

## Dependence

The most important biological change is functional dependence.

The resident's body becomes increasingly suited to:

-   Scheduled meals
-   Extended rest
-   Environmental regulation
-   External assistance
-   Managed movement
-   Continuous monitoring

The Centre eventually becomes not merely where the resident lives, but
the environment their altered physiology expects.
