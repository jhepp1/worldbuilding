# AC Cognitive Changes

AC gradually changes cognition and behavior.

The process is usually presented as stabilization rather than
punishment.

## Common Progression

### Stage 1 --- Independent

The resident remains largely unchanged.

### Stage 2 --- Supported

They accept more assistance and monitoring.

### Stage 3 --- Gentle

Aggression, suspicion, and argumentative behavior are reduced.

### Stage 4 --- Dependent

The resident increasingly relies on staff and systems for decisions.

### Stage 5 --- Fragile

The resident has extensive cognitive dependence and very limited
independent planning.

## Behavioral Characteristics

Long-term residents may become:

-   Extremely agreeable
-   Playful
-   Affectionate
-   Easily distracted
-   Sleepy
-   Dependent
-   Reassurance-seeking
-   Less interested in complex decisions
-   Less aware of their own decline

Eli is highly playful and affectionate by this stage.

Theo initially retains much more independence and cognitive sharpness,
but eventually undergoes similar changes.
