The high lord glacius is a moth (at least in taranvesh) blessed with the aspect of frost and one of the higher beings in my multiverse known as the great observer to many who worship it ,

it is said to watch over the weak and poor , temples of worship for Glacius are filled with abundant  and often foreign life brought from across the multiverse it looks out of place as it draws followers to build temples at the highest regions (for taranvesh specifically it is the [[asgear mountain range ]]) between [[Mastonia]] and [[Rustionia]] and [[Tavas]] legends also state it is responsible for the [[veil between worlds]] in rustionia allowing bugs from all over taranvesh to travel the multiverse / [[stovangrad]] leading to much chaos and leading to bugs being the first multiversal species although their sentience is highly diminished as the runes are severely weakened across the web of [[stovangrad]] 

as a deity Glacius is more nurturing than its siblings often detecting the good in people and not requiring much to be sacrificed  in return for its support / aid 

on the minimal list of offerings that glacius (optionally) receives for aid it prefers fresh fruit and flowers scholars from [[Mastonia]] theorise that it decorates its dwelling in stovangrad and eats the fresh fruit  

the denizens of taranvesh show their worship by carving wooden pendents and totems as they do with all higher beings for Glacius in particular they decorate the totems with coals or sapphires and a light blue stain made of indigo mites and bones of the biped which makes it a valuable and highly prized pigment  for most nations 

its true form is said to have 1000 eyes  each one glowing with the light of several universes
