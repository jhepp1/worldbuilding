# Daily Life in AC

AC residents live on highly structured routines.

A typical day for Eli might look like:

-   **08:00** --- Wake
-   **09:00** --- Rest
-   **10:00** --- Gentle activity
-   **11:00** --- Rest
-   **12:30** --- Lunch
-   **13:30** --- Extended afternoon rest
-   **15:30** --- Social time
-   **17:00** --- Quiet activity
-   **18:30** --- Dinner
-   **19:30** --- Evening relaxation
-   **21:00** --- Bathing and preparation
-   **22:00** --- Sleep

The schedule is flexible in appearance but heavily managed underneath.

## Rest

Rest is considered a legitimate form of care.

Eli is almost permanently heavy-eyed and can fall asleep in many places.

## Play

The Centre provides:

-   Games
-   Consoles
-   Puzzles
-   Gardens
-   Interactive displays
-   Music
-   Crafts
-   Social activities

Eli is particularly playful and easily entertained.

## Food

Meals are a major source of comfort and anticipation.

## Social Life

Residents can form close friendships and relationships.

The system monitors these relationships and may alter accommodation or
schedules if it believes social arrangements improve wellbeing.
