# Centre Food System

The Centre's food is exceptionally appealing.

The kitchen is closer to a laboratory than a conventional kitchen.

## Kitchen Infrastructure

The food system contains:

-   Automated preparation chambers
-   Ingredient reservoirs
-   Food printers
-   Analytical equipment
-   Robotic preparation systems
-   Automated ovens
-   Sensory analysis systems
-   Resident preference databases

Few conventional cooks are needed.

## Fictional Food Chemistry

The Centre uses proprietary fictional substances including:

-   **Sensory Harmonizers** --- alter perceived aroma, flavour, and
    texture.
-   **Reward Stabilizers** --- strengthen positive associations between
    eating and comfort.

The precise chemistry is inaccessible to most staff.

## Personalization

The system tracks:

-   Favourite flavours
-   Texture preferences
-   Temperature preferences
-   Meal completion
-   Positive emotional responses
-   Anticipation
-   Post-meal satisfaction

It then continually modifies future meals.

## AC Residents

AC residents respond especially strongly because their altered biology
and embedded systems are designed to interact with Centre environmental
technology.

For ordinary visitors, the food is simply unusually good.

For AC residents, eating can become deeply associated with comfort,
safety, familiarity, and belonging.

The Centre therefore creates a feedback loop:

**Resident likes food → system records response → system improves food →
resident likes it more.**

The residents generally interpret this simply as having excellent food.
