Time traveller from the year 4022, he is responsible for most disasters in history after trying to meet his heroes. He is very unlucky; he is responsible for disasters such as: 

The Hindenburg

Nazi Germany

911.

The Great Fire of London

The Berlin Wall

The list goes on

He accidentally time travelled to 1961 America and was found in a Murkoff trial and was subsequently hunted by [[the maid]], who has a murderous disposition. She later became his wife after many awkward dates and encounters.

They have two children, [[squimbus]] and [[throckmorton]] together, although how they came into existence is a mystery,  as Dave and his darling wife say it was fate’s hand, whatever that means. 

He is chaotic good, often trying to do the right thing in the strangest way possible, but he has a heart of gold; friends often describe him lovingly as a chaos goblin. 

Due to his abilities, he is one of the few people to be aware of the multiverse and some of the worlds inside it; he is particularly fond of the [[Troilia]] marshlands in [[taranvesh]] , though he is giant compared to everything it contains.
