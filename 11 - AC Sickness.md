# AC Sickness

AC facilities contain fictional environmental nanotechnology that
interacts with resident identification systems.

Registered residents receive the full environmental configuration.

Visitors experience a milder version.

## Stages

### Stage 1 --- Settling

-   Calmness
-   Sleepiness
-   Reduced urgency
-   Altered sense of time

### Stage 2 --- Softening

-   Increased agreeableness
-   Less urgency
-   Desire for staff assistance
-   Reduced desire to leave

### Stage 3 --- Disorientation

-   Time confusion
-   Forgotten intentions
-   Reliance on familiar guidance
-   Outside world begins to feel hectic

### Stage 4 --- Attachment

-   Reluctance to leave
-   Trust in Centre staff
-   Preference for Centre routines

### Stages 5--6

Severe dependence and difficulty organizing departure.

Staff intervene if necessary.

### Stage 7 --- Claiming

Extremely rare.

A visitor who remains too long and repeatedly fails to leave may be
classified by the system as a potential resident.

The Centre treats this as safeguarding rather than punishment.

Theo is one of the rare people to experience this process.
