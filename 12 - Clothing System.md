# Clothing System

AC clothing is luxurious, comfortable, standardized, and deliberately
impersonal.

## Layers

### Base Layer

Soft seamless undershirt.

Available in short- and long-sleeved versions.

### Main Layer

Simple facility shirts in pale colours.

### Tunic

The recognizable pale-blue AC tunic.

Loose, smooth, and designed for easy care.

### Trousers

Matching elastic-waist trousers.

No belts or complicated fasteners.

### Cardigan

Soft pale cardigan used for warmth and comfort.

### Socks

Seamless white or pale socks.

### Footwear

Soft slip-on indoor shoes and specialized outdoor footwear.

### Outerwear

Lightweight facility coats for rain, winter, and outdoor activities.

### Sleepwear

Loose sleep shirt, soft trousers, warm socks, and robe.

## Loss of Choice

Residents technically have clothing options.

However, the system increasingly selects clothing automatically based
on:

-   Weather
-   Activity
-   Mobility
-   Temperature
-   Care requirements
-   Resident preferences

If a resident resists, the system can move them into assisted-care
dressing.

The unsettling feature is that the clothing is genuinely comfortable.

The loss of autonomy is therefore disguised by convenience.
