# National Allowance

The **National Allowance** is a universal personal spending allocation
provided to citizens.

A nominal adult allowance is approximately **£1,200 per month** in
contemporary-equivalent terms.

However, money is not central to everyday survival.

## Covered Separately

The following are broadly provided through public systems:

-   Housing
-   Food
-   Healthcare
-   Education
-   Basic transport
-   Utilities
-   Basic clothing

The allowance is mainly used for discretionary spending:

-   Hobbies
-   Entertainment
-   Gifts
-   Optional travel
-   Cafés
-   Personal luxuries
-   Private purchases

## AC Residents

AC residents technically continue to receive the allowance.

However, most spending is managed by the Centre.

Eli may have substantial funds credited to his account while barely
thinking about money at all.

The Centre can purchase nearly everything he needs automatically.

This makes money administratively important but psychologically distant
from daily life.
