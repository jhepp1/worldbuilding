# Artificial Care

**Artificial Care (AC)** is a state-run system for citizens classified
as requiring behavioral intervention after serious or repeated
rule-breaking.

AC is publicly described as compassionate rehabilitation.

It provides residents with:

-   Luxury accommodation
-   Food and personal care
-   Healthcare
-   Clothing
-   Recreation
-   Education and activities
-   Pain management
-   Constant monitoring
-   Social support
-   A managed National Allowance

## The Hidden Cost

AC gradually changes residents physically and cognitively.

The system can:

-   Reduce independence
-   Increase reliance on support workers
-   Alter behavior
-   Rewrite aspects of biology
-   Modify sleep and wakefulness
-   Change appetite and digestion
-   Alter emotional responses
-   Reduce resistance to care
-   Reorganize memories
-   Make the Centre feel more familiar than the outside world

Residents generally do not perceive these changes accurately.

The Centre modifies their ability to recognize the transformation
itself.

## AC Philosophy

The system does not regard itself as cruel.

Its internal logic is:

> If a resident is safe, comfortable, healthy, and happy, then
> intervention has succeeded.

Autonomy is treated as valuable only insofar as it contributes to
wellbeing.

## Reintegration

Rare residents may eventually be considered suitable for supervised
reintegration.

However, physical and cognitive changes are usually permanent.

Reintegration is therefore uncommon.
